## 完整描述（两平台通用）

### Insurance Profile — your policies, all in one place

Insurance Profile is a private, local-first insurance wallet for Australia. It helps you
keep track of every policy — car, home, landlord, health and pet — in one place, and
never miss a renewal again.

**Everything on your device**

Your policy details, documents and extraction results are stored only on your phone.
Nothing is uploaded to a server, sold or shared with third parties. Enable the optional
App Lock (biometrics or device passcode) for extra protection.

**Never miss an expiry**

Set your Policy Expiry Date and receive local reminders 30, 15, 7 and 3 days before it
expires. When you renew, the reminders automatically follow your new policy.

**Upload and auto-fill**

Upload a renewal notice or policy PDF — the app reads it on your device and fills in the
details for you. No typing required for the common fields.

**See what actually changed**

Compare this year's policy with last year's: premium changes (amount and percentage),
car cover details (roadside assistance, windscreen, replacement value) and home/landlord
insured amounts.

**One wallet for all your insurance**

Add multiple policies for each type — multiple cars, properties and pets. Health
insurance is kept simple with one active policy.

**Make a Claim in one tap**

Jump straight to your insurer's official claims page from the policy details screen.

**Private by design**

- Data stays on your device
- No account, no server, no tracking
- Optional biometric App Lock
- Export and restore your own backup anytime

Insurance Profile is an information and record-keeping tool. It does not provide
insurance advice or recommendations. All quote figures shown in the app are example
data unless sourced from documents you provided. Always review your policy's Product
Disclosure Statement before making decisions.

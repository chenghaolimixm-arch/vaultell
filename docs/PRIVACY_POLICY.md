## 2. Information we collect

The App lets you keep a personal record of your insurance policies. The information you choose to enter includes:

- **Profile information:** your name and other details you add to your local profile.
- **Policy subjects:** details about your vehicles, properties and pets that you associate with policies.
- **Policy information:** insurer, policy number, cover type, premium, excess, insured amounts, cover features (such as roadside assistance and windscreen cover), policy expiry date, status and notes.
- **Historical policy information:** details you enter about your previous year's policy, used to show year-over-year comparisons.
- **Quote information:** quotes you add or import, including premium, excess and cover summary.
- **Documents:** policy documents, renewal notices and quote files you upload. These files are stored on your device. Information extracted from them (for example premium or expiry date) is stored locally as structured data.
- **Device preference:** whether you have enabled the in-app lock.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos library, browsing history, or any other data beyond what you explicitly enter or upload into the App.

## 3. How we use your information

Your information is used only to provide the App's features:

- Storing and displaying your insurance records.
- Reminding you before your policies expire (local notifications).
- Comparing your current policy with the previous year's details you entered.
- Importing and auto-filling policy details from documents you upload.
- Enabling the optional app lock.

We do **not** use your information for advertising, marketing, or analytics, and we do **not** sell or rent your information to anyone.

## 4. Where your information is stored

All information is stored **on your device** in the App's private storage. There is no server, cloud account or third-party database involved in the standard use of the App.

If you use the optional **backup export** feature, a JSON backup file containing your policy records is created by you and shared through your device's share sheet (for example, saved to Files, emailed to yourself, or stored in your own cloud drive). That backup file is under your control and is not transmitted to us.

## 5. How we protect your information

We apply the following protections:

- Data is stored in the App's private sandboxed storage on your device.
- iOS and Android device encryption protects data at rest.
- The optional **App Lock** requires biometrics or your device passcode before the App's content can be opened.
- No information is transmitted over the network in standard use.

While no storage method is 100% secure, keeping data on your device removes the risks associated with centralised servers.

## 6. How long we keep your information and how to delete it

- Information stays on your device for as long as you keep the App and your records.
- You can delete individual policies, files and quotes at any time from within the App.
- To delete **all** data, uninstall the App or use your device's option to clear the App's data. This permanently removes your local records.
- Backup files you created yourself (for example, exported JSON files) are not controlled by the App once shared; delete them from the locations where you saved them if you no longer need them.

## 7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not share your information with any third party, and we do not sell it.

Two optional features involve information leaving your device, and only when you choose to act:

- **Opening an insurer's claim page:** tapping "Make a Claim" opens the insurer's website in your browser. Any information you enter there is subject to that insurer's own privacy policy.
- **Future cloud features:** if cloud-based document analysis or encrypted backup is added in a future version, it will be introduced only with your explicit, per-document consent, end-to-end encryption where applicable, and an update to this policy before release.

## 8. Your rights under Australian privacy law

The App is designed to comply with the **Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)** and the **Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)**. Because your information stays on your device, you control it directly. You can:

- Access and correct your information at any time by editing records in the App.
- Delete individual records or all data as described in section 6.
- Contact us with any privacy request or complaint using the details in section 1.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the **Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)** at [www.oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au).
